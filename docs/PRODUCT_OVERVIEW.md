# Product Overview — Universal Personal Medical AI

## 1. Product Statement
**"Your Entire Medical Life. One Intelligent Platform."**

The **Universal Personal Medical AI** is an intelligent, lifelong digital health companion that empowers individuals to store, understand, track, and act on their complete medical history. Rather than serving as an administrative portal for hospital billing or a doctor practice management SaaS, it is 100% patient-centered and patient-owned.

---

## 2. Target User Cohorts
1. **Healthy Individuals**: Storing baseline lab results, immunization cards, health measurements, and preventive reminders.
2. **Acute Care Patients**: Navigating short-term illnesses, surgeries, symptom trajectories, and antibiotic courses.
3. **Chronic Condition Patients**: Managing multi-drug polypharmacy, daily glucose/blood pressure tracking, specialist visits, and longitudinal disease progression.
4. **Caregivers & Family Proxies**: Overseeing children or elderly parents with segregated data isolation and explicit delegation.

---

## 3. Core Functional Pillars
- **Medical Vault**: Secure multi-format document ingestion (PDFs, images) with OCR extraction, classification, and Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) verification.
- **Lifelong Medical Timeline**: Unified chronological record of diagnoses, surgeries, doctor consults, medications, and labs.
- **Medication Intelligence**: Prescription parsing, daily dosing schedules, refill warnings, and adherence tracking.
- **Biometric Analytics**: Visual trends for Blood Pressure, Blood Glucose, HbA1c, Heart Rate, and Weight with clinical reference intervals.
- **Context-Aware Medical AI**: Grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assistant with mandatory source attribution and emergency safety guardrails.
- **Smart Follow-Ups**: Proactive check-in engine for treatment completion and post-consultation recovery.
- **Clinical Summary & Sharing**: One-click print-ready clinical summaries and time-limited, encrypted access tokens for doctor appointments.
